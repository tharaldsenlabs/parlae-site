What Parlae Does

Parlae is an iPhone and macOS application with voice conversation, an animated avatar, identity, history, memory, and supported tools. It can operate independently on an iPhone or connect to a Parlae identity running on your Mac. Configured model and speech providers may process requests over the internet. Mac configurations can also delegate authorized work to Codex or Claude Code and connect to Loopwright for creative workflows.
