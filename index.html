Parlae

Your AI companion on iPhone and Mac. Talk naturally, with continuity across conversations.

Parlae brings a persistent AI identity to life through voice, an animated avatar, and memory you control. Choose your model and voice, hold a hands-free conversation, and give your identity permission to use tools that help with everyday tasks.

Independent on iPhone

Use Parlae on your iPhone with its own identity, conversation history, memory, and device tools. Cloud models connect over the internet while the phone manages your context and permissions. Supported tools include web search, Calendar, Reminders, and connected email accounts.

Connect your Mac

For desktop capabilities, connect the same iPhone app to a Parlae identity running on your Mac. The Mac manages that identity and its tools, including authorized access to workspaces and applications. You can talk to it from your iPhone or directly on the Mac.

Parlae is under active development and private testing. Developed by Tharaldsen Labs, LLC in North Carolina, USA.
